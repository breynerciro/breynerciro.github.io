About Me

I am a Systems and Computing Engineer from Universidad Nacional de Colombia. I build SaaS products end to end: Vue and React frontends with TypeScript, Node.js and Python backends, and data in PostgreSQL, Firestore and BigQuery.

At Kila I build AI agents (Claude, Gemini, MCP) that work with real foreign-trade data. On my own I develop BreVenta, a point of sale with electronic invoicing, and Denty, a platform for dental practices that is already in production.
